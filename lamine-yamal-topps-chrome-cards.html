What Drives Lamine's Card Value

Value is driven by his 2023-24 Topps Chrome UCC first-year autos (the /5 Red and SuperFractor 1/1s lead), his 2024 Topps Chrome UEFA Euro autographs, and Sapphire parallels. Grade matters enormously: PSA 10 / Auto 10 copies command multiples of raw, and serial-numbered parallels behave as distinct cards rather than condition-graded versions of one.

FAQ

What is Lamine Yamal's most valuable card?
His record public sale is a 2024 Topps Chrome UEFA Euro Autographs SuperFractor 1/1 PSA 10 at $396,500.
What is Lamine Yamal's rookie card?
His flagship rookie autos come from 2023-24 Topps Chrome UEFA Club Competitions (first UCC RC autos) and 2024 Topps Chrome UEFA Euro.
Are Lamine Yamal cards a good investment?
His sales are among the strongest in the category, but prices on 1/1s and low-numbered parallels are volatile — this page tracks live comps so you can judge the current market, not a snapshot.
