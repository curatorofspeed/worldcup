What Drives Erling's Card Value

His 2019-20 Bundesliga rookie autos (especially the SuperFractor and low-numbered refractors) drive the top of the market, with UCL and Premier League issues from his Manchester City move adding demand. Condition and serial numbering are the two biggest price levers.

FAQ

What is Erling Haaland's most valuable card?
A 2019-20 Topps Chrome Bundesliga Autographs SuperFractor 1/1 BGS 9.5 sold for $610,000.
What is Haaland's rookie card?
His key rookies are the 2019-20 Topps Chrome Bundesliga autographs from his breakout Salzburg/Dortmund season.
Why are Haaland's cards so expensive?
Generational goal-scoring output plus a relatively thin print run of his earliest Topps Chrome autos concentrates demand on a small number of graded copies.
