What Drives Kylian's Card Value

Demand centers on his earliest 2017-18 rookies and premium inserts (Kaboom!, Flawless, National Treasures), plus modern Topps Chrome UCC autos. As an established global star, his blue-chip 1/1s hold value more steadily than younger speculative names.
